# OCBC Steady — The Autonomous Paycheck for Singapore's Gig Economy
**PolyFinTech100 API Hackathon 2026 · Intelligent Banking (OCBC)**

Singapore's 70,500 platform workers — private-hire drivers, taxi drivers, delivery riders — earn daily but live in a monthly world of rent, bills and, since the Platform Workers Act took effect in January 2025, mandatory CPF contributions rising to 20% of income by 2029. They have no payroll department: each worker must manually smooth volatile earnings, set aside CPF and tax, and build savings — and without a payslip, they remain invisible to credit and home financing.

OCBC Steady is an autonomous AI agent that becomes their payroll department. It observes earnings as they land and allocates every dollar down a deterministic ladder: CPF/MediSave and tax set-asides first, then a pre-funded bills envelope, then a fixed weekly "Steady Salary" paid into the worker's spending account, then a four-week buffer, then surplus swept toward goals. Good weeks fund the buffer; lean weeks draw on it invisibly — the salary always arrives.

Users stay in control through a personal "Constitution": Steady acts alone beneath user-set caps and only between the user's own OCBC accounts, asks first above them, and never moves money outside those accounts. Every action produces a plain-language receipt with a 24-hour undo.

Phase 1 is deliberately buildable: cash-only sweeps across OCBC 360 and savings pockets via OCBC's open APIs — no investment licensing required. Later phases add platform payout integrations, SGFinDex, and a Steady income record accepted as a payslip-equivalent for loans. Where OCBC WoW brings agentic banking to the wealthiest 1%, Steady extends the same AI-native architecture to the workers who keep Singapore moving — converting a new regulatory burden into the stickiest mass-market banking relationship in Singapore.
